Quiet chrome. The artwork is the colour.

One system for the Creative Editor's shell and the Pattern Studio: white cards on a cool grey ground, a single black ink, one family of type at seven sizes, and every control the same 30 px tall. The studio it is measured from does one thing well — it gets out of the way of the pattern — and that is the whole brief for the editor's chrome too.

Principles

Five rules. If a screen breaks one, the screen is wrong.

The pattern owns colour.Chrome is grey, white and black. The only saturated colour in the interface is the canvas selection blue, and it never leaves the canvas.
One control height.Buttons, fields, steppers, chips and switches are all 30 px tall (22 for the switch). Rows line up because nothing is taller than the next thing.
Radius by role.7 for fields, 9 for icon buttons, 11 for calls to action, 12 for cards and menus, 14 for the board, 16 for panels. A radius says what kind of object you are looking at.
A number is a chip.Values sit in a black chip beside their label. Sliders open on demand; counts are steppers. The panel shows what a thing is, not a row of tracks.
Say what it costs."9 handles." "Mean error 4.6/255, close." Copy names the consequence of a control, in sentence case, without exclamation marks.

Colour

The neutrals lean cool — the ground is #F2F2F4, not #F2F2F2 — so the artwork's warm colours read warmer against it. Every alpha is a fraction of the ink, which keeps hairlines and borders the same hue as the text they sit beside.

Type

One family: the system UI face — SF Pro Text on Apple machines, Inter (loaded) elsewhere — ui-sans-serif, -apple-system, "SF Pro Text", "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif. Seven roles, all between 9 and 22 px. Digits are tabular everywhere, so a value chip does not jitter while a slider moves.

Icons

Lucide, vendored in icons.js. 16 px in rows and menus, 18 px in the tool rail, 1.75 stroke, round caps, currentColor. Idle icons are muted; the active tool is white on ink; nothing is ever blue.

tool rail · 18 px · active = ink
Every icon the editor ships, by its data-icon name. New icons come from Lucide at the same stroke; an icon drawn by hand is the tell of a different system.

Colour picker

Replaces the browser's popup, which ignores the system. It unfolds in three steps — the pill header, the swatch row, the picker — and the swatch row keeps what you press + on. Try it: drag the square, the hue and the alpha; type a hex; press +.

Open. 272 wide, panel radius 16, popover shadow. Header: eyedropper (only where the browser has one), swatches toggle, tune toggle, and the hex in a 34 px pill field with the colour as a dot. Square 176 tall with a 14 px ring cursor; hue and alpha bars 12 tall with 16 px thumbs; the alpha bar sits on a 10 px checkerboard. Eight-digit hex when alpha is below 100%.
Swatches only and collapsed — the tune and swatches buttons fold the picker down to what a quick pick needs. Cells are 7-radius squares with a 1 px inner hairline; the current colour carries a white dot; the last cell is the rainbow +.
Colour
In a row. A colour control is a 30 px pill with the dot and the hex. Click it and the picker opens in a popover under it (flipping above when there is no room); a click outside or Escape closes it. Drag fires input, release fires change — the artwork redraws live and the history gets one step.
  • Hex is the value. RGB/HSL fields are not shown; the hex field accepts 3, 4, 6 and 8 digits with or without the hash and puts the last good value back if you leave it broken.
  • Grey keeps its hue. Typing #888 does not swing the hue bar to red; the square stays where it was so the next drag starts from a sensible place.
  • Keyboard. The square and both bars take arrow keys (shift for ×5). The whole picker is one tab stop per surface.
  • Swatches persist per app under a storage key; the defaults are the system's eight.

Motion and voice

Motion. One duration: 120 ms, ease. Hover fills, switch knobs and section chevrons use it; nothing bounces, nothing fades in on scroll. Panels do not animate open. prefers-reduced-motion collapses every transition to nothing.

Live values. A slider redraws on every input and commits one history step on change; a drag on the canvas is the same. The value chip updates with the pointer; the artwork never lags the chip.

Sentence case. Labels, buttons and menus are sentence case ("Match an image…", "Show net while selected"); only section heads and the CTA are uppercase, and the stylesheet does that, not the copy.

Say the cost, then the promise. "9 handles. Resizing resamples the surface, so nothing is thrown away." Numbers first, consequence second, no adjectives.

Report measurements, not confidence. "Matched — mean colour error 4.6/255 (close)." A fit that missed says so: "far off — try more columns and rows."

Errors name the fix. "AI not configured — add GROQ_API_KEY to .env and restart the server." Never "Something went wrong."

Shortcuts are shown in menus as ⌘Z, right-aligned, muted, and nowhere else.

Ellipsis means a dialog or picker follows ("Match an image…"); its absence means the action happens now ("Reset net").
